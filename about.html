Who We Are

Built for E-commerce Growth, Since 2023

Atomic Ads was founded in 2023 with a single focus: helping brands win on the platforms that drive real revenue. From Amazon and Flipkart to Meta and Google, our team combines deep marketplace expertise with a data-first approach to advertising.

In a short span, we've helped our clients generate over ₹30 Cr+ in revenue, backed by rigorous campaign optimization, listing strategy and account management across seven major platforms.
